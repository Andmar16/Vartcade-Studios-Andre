Turn flow, motion and platform
Turn flow
YOUR TURN stamp, then pick an action, then the card or target picker opens (board 06 / pickers), confirm, animation, 'Turn over' toast, and the next player's spotlight.
Motion
Scavenge: card flies from the pile, flips to its front, slides into the fan (0.35 s). Trade sent: card slides toward the target and hovers until accepted or bounced back. Opponent reactions: shocked face when they lose a card.
Phone vs PC
Phone: action bar on the right for the right thumb; hand cards at least 96 px wide; long-press = tooltip. PC: hover = tooltip; number keys 1 to 3 trigger actions (Could).
5 or 6 players
Opponents move into an arc of 4 or 5; puppets scale to 0.9; their hands show as small stacks with a count, tap to expand.
Notes for developers
Game table: Day phase, your turn
Assumptions on this screen: You are not drawn at the table; a portrait shows your expressions (D-Q4). 4-player table shown. Timer 30 s (D-Q1).
1
Environment: bunker room (animated)
Wall, shelf of supplies, hanging lamp, window. The window sky darkens a step each Day (Day 1 orange afternoon, Day 10 almost black) so time pressure is visible without reading. Lamp sways gently; dust drifts in the light cone.
New: TableBackdrop (rework) · Should (A10)
2
Opponents as puppets across the table
Placeholder puppets with seat numbers, seated behind the far edge (Buckshot Roulette framing). 4 players: left, center, right. 5 to 6 players: they shift into an arc. Name plate shows card count. Puppets idle (breathe, blink) and react.
PlayerZoneController · Must (layout) / C04 idle
3
Opponents' card fronts are visible
Rule: fronts are public, backs are hidden. Each opponent's hand lies face-up (fronts) in front of them, small. Tapping a card opens the close-up (board 06). The Bunker Key front looks like a Supply front, so nothing here can reveal it.
PlayerZoneController + CardView · Must
4
Table center: draw pile and Rondo
Draw pile with a count. Rondo dial sits greyed out until Day 6, then lights up and spins at the start of each Day (board 07).
DrawPileController, RondoDial · Must
5
Day counter and phase
DAY 3 of 10, a dot per Day, phase tag (orange DAY / teal NIGHT). If the Doomsday Clock is active, a red clock replaces the dots of the cut Days.
HUDController · Must
6
Bunker seats and pause
Seat icons (2 or 3). Pause opens Settings, How to Play and Leave.
HUDController, PauseManager · Must
7
Kinds tracker (most important new HUD)
Shows which of the 4 kinds you hold and the 3/3 goal; bar fills; border turns gold at 3. Losing for missing kinds is now the top cause of losses (37 to 44%), so this must always be visible. Key holders see a gold key line instead.
New: KindsTracker in HUDController · Must (L04)
8
YOUR TURN popup and timer
Stamps in with a small rotation and a thud, stays 1.2 s, then shrinks into the timer ring beside it. Ring drains over 30 s; turns red under 5 s with ticks.
TurnBanner + HUDController · Must
9
Your hand (fanned, UNO style)
Cards fan along the bottom. Hover or long-press lifts a card and opens its tooltip. Selected card glows gold. Played Morning cards stay in hand, greyed with a USED stamp.
HandController · Must
10
Card tooltip
Name, what it does in one or two lines, and what the back means at Sundown (hidden for you only if unknown). Same component as the Card Gallery.
CardView + new Tooltip · Must (L07)
11
Your portrait
You are not seated on screen (first-person view). Your puppet head sits in a gold ring and shows your expression: thinking on your turn, shocked when you lose a card. Taunts play here too.
New: PortraitView · Should (C03)
12
Action bar
Day: 1 action. Buttons: Play card (only if you hold an unused Morning card), Trade (pick their card by its front, then wait for accept), Scavenge (draw 1). Disabled buttons stay visible with the reason on hover or tap. The turn ends by itself when the action is used.
ActionBarController · Must (L05)
13
Table perspective
Tilted trapezoid with a perspective camera (task F07). If the 3D camera costs too much on phones, the fallback is this same image painted flat.
BoardController + camera · Must
