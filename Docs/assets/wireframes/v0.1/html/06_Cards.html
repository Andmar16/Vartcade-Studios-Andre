Tooltip, motion and sizes
Tooltip (UNO style)
Hover on PC or long-press (0.35 s) on phone shows: name, kind or type, one-line effect, and what its back does at Sundown if you know it. Closes on release or when the pointer leaves.
Motion
Flip: scale X to 0 then back with the other face (0.25 s). Draw: slide from the pile with a slight arc. USED stamp: thumps down with a little shake.
Sizes
Hand: 104 x 146 at 720p (at least 96 px wide on phone). Opponent hand: 46 x 64. Close-up: 210 x 300. Keep the 0.7 ratio everywhere.
Art pipeline
One sprite per front and back; frame, band and text built in the prefab so text stays sharp and translatable.
Notes for developers
Cards: anatomy, backs and states
1
Supply front anatomy
Color band + shape symbol (Food circle, Water drop, Medicine plus, Entertainment diamond), illustration slot, kind name. The shape must be readable at 46 px wide (opponent hands), so keep symbols bold and simple.
CardView · Must (A01, A03)
2
Four Supply kinds
The same four symbols appear on the HUD kinds tracker and on the results screen, so players learn them once. Colors from the merged palette; never rely on color alone.
CardView + HUD · Must
3
Morning fronts
Charcoal header with 'MORNING' in gold, the card name, and an icon (art slot). Tooltip gives the effect. A played Morning card stays in hand with the USED stamp.
CardView · Must
4
Backs
Supply, Trash, Flashlight, Night Skip, Day Skip each need a distinct color and symbol, because Peek and Flashlight reveal them quickly. The Bunker Key's FRONT must look exactly like a Supply front; only its back is special (art TBD).
CardView · Must (A02)
5
States
Normal, hover (lift 18 px, scale 1.05), selected (gold outline + glow), USED (grey veil + red stamp, rotated), peeked (teal eye badge, only for you), not playable (40% opacity, tooltip says why).
CardView · Must (L06)
6
Face-down
Sundown emblem on a patterned dark back. Used for the draw pile and for any card whose front you should not see (e.g. Swap picks).
CardView · Must
