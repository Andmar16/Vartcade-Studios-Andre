States, motion and platform
States
Buttons: normal, hover (PC), pressed, disabled. Hover: tilt 2 degrees, scale 1.03, paper rustle sound. Pressed: moves down 4 px onto its shadow.
Motion and sound
Ambient loop: wind, distant siren every 40 to 60 s, bulb buzz. Screen change: the camera pans down into the hatch (0.6 s) then fades to the next scene.
Phone vs PC
Phone: buttons on the left half so thumbs reach them; puppet and hatch stay right. PC: same layout; mouse parallax on. Safe area: keep the left 60 px clear of the logo on notch phones.
Reduce motion
Layers stop moving; ash particles hidden; bulb stays steady on; menu still fully usable.
Notes for developers
Main Menu with animated environment
1
Animated environment background (layered parallax)
6 sprite layers, back to front: sky gradient (slow color breathing), clouds (drift left, 90 s loop), sun (sinks 20 px and rises again over 60 s), far ruins silhouette, ground with dead tree, foreground ash particles. Each layer moves at its own speed; on PC the mouse adds 5 to 15 px of parallax, on phone the gyroscope is optional (Could).
New: MenuBackground.cs · Should (A09) · Loop must be seamless
2
Bunker hatch and flickering bulb
The bunker is the goal, so it is the brightest point on the right. Bulb flickers at random intervals (never faster than 3 flashes per second, for photosensitive players). Hatch lid rattles slightly every 8 to 12 s.
Part of MenuBackground · Should · Flicker off when Reduce Motion is on
3
Logo and tagline
TEN DAYS in cream, TO SUNDOWN in gold, hard charcoal shadow. Tagline in Special Elite. Logo drops in with a small bounce on first load only.
MainMenuController · Must (title) / Should (final logo art A07)
4
Play online (primary)
Opens a small modal: Host game (creates a join code, goes to Lobby) or Join (6-character code field). Biggest button because it is the main path.
MainMenuController + SessionManager · Must
5
Play vs bots
Goes straight to a Lobby filled with bots; player picks table size 4 to 6 and bot style (Aggressive, Passive, Human-like, Random).
MainMenuController · Must
6
Tutorial (new)
Gold so new players notice it. Opens the guided game (W15). Shows a NEW tag until the tutorial is finished once.
MainMenuController · Must (button) / Should (guided content)
7
Secondary row: Cards, Profile, Settings; Quit
Cards = Card Gallery scene. Profile = wins, streaks, recent results. Settings = audio, text size, colorblind mode, reduce motion. Quit only on PC builds (hidden on phone).
MenuScreens · Must

A placeholder puppet (thin body, seat 01) stands by the bunker: breathes, blinks, looks toward the cursor or the last tapped button, occasionally checks a wristwatch. Later it shows the player's own avatar.
