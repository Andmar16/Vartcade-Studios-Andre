Flow, motion and platform
Flow
Dawn transition, then the room dims, the wheel rises from the table center, spins, the result lands, the effect happens (cards fly to the discard), the wheel sinks back, and the Day starts.
Motion
Wheel rises 0.4 s, spins 3 to 4 s, result banner stamps in. Cards removed by Purge or Hunger fly from hands to the center and burn away.
Phone vs PC
Same. The wheel is at least 360 px across on phones so slice names stay readable.
Note
Water is not used by any Rondo result (open question in the project doc). Worth checking before final slice art.
Notes for developers
Rondo: spin and result
1
The wheel
Appears from Day 6, at the start of each Day, before anyone acts. 8 slices from the rules; Peaceful Day is a thinner slice because it is rare. Slice art: an icon per result (A06).
RondoDial · Must (show) / Should (restyle)
2
Pointer and spin
Spin lasts 3 to 4 s with an ease-out and a tick per slice passed; the last ticks slow down for tension. The result is decided by the host before the spin; the animation only shows it.
RondoDial · Should (C11)
3
Result banner
Name, one-line effect, and what it did to YOU ('You lose: Food'). Stays until everyone taps OK or 5 s pass. Color: orange for disasters, gold for Peaceful Day, dark red for Doomsday.
RondoDial + TurnBanner · Must
4
Doomsday Clock
When it lands, the game is shortened. HUD day dots after the new last Day turn red; a clock ticks in the HUD from then on. It cannot land on the last Day (patch notes).
HUDController · Must
5
Environment reaction
Each result shakes the room a little differently: Tornado (lamp swings hard, dust falls), Blackout (lights cut out for the Day), Hunger (stomach growl sound, puppets look worried), Outbreak (green tint pulse).
TableBackdrop · Could (C12) · Off with Reduce Motion
