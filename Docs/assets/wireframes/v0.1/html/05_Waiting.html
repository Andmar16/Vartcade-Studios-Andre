Why, motion and timer rules
Why this matters
Players wait 8 to 14 minutes a game (patch notes). This screen is seen more than any other, so it must stay alive: spotlight, live status, readable hand, emotes.
Motion
Active puppet: small thinking loop (scratch head, tap chin). When their action lands: their puppet reacts (happy or shocked) and a short line appears in the banner.
Timer at zero (assumption)
Day: the turn ends with no action. Night: if no Peek was done, a random Peek is done automatically, then the turn ends. Waiting on D-Q1.
Phone vs PC
Phone: banner shrinks to one line at the top; emotes collapse into one button that opens a small wheel.
Notes for developers
Waiting screen (Day) and blackout (Night)
Assumptions on this screen: Timer at zero ends the turn; a Peek is done for you at Night if needed (D-Q1).
1
Whose turn banner with timer
Name, countdown (Day 30 s, Night 20 s), progress bar, and a live line of what they are doing ('Choosing a trade', 'Scavenging', 'Playing Reverse Turn'). Shows who is next so you can get ready.
New: WaitingView (uses IGameView events) · Must (L08)
2
Spotlight on the active player
The scene darkens except a soft cone on the active puppet, which shows its thinking face and '...' bubble. Their name plate turns orange.
PlayerZoneController + TableBackdrop · Must
3
Turn order list
Who already played, who is now, who is next. Reverse Turn flips the list with a short animation.
HUDController · Should
4
Your hand while waiting
Slightly desaturated so it is clearly not your turn, but you can still hover or long-press every card to read it and plan.
HandController · Must
5
Emotes
Angry, giddy, sad, thinking. Your portrait and your seat (on other screens) play the expression and a short pose. 5 s cooldown so nobody spams.
New: EmoteBar · Could (C07) · Network message
6
Incoming trade prompt
Trades now pick a card by its front, so the prompt says exactly which cards. Accept or Refuse; refusing tells the other player clearly (refused trades happen about 3 times a game).
CardPicker / trade flow · Must (U02)
7
Night version: eyes closed screen
During other players' Night turns, everything is hidden: black screen, closed eyes, 'Someone is moving in the dark', a timer bar. No names, no hands, nothing that could leak. Your own phone vibrates lightly when it is your Night turn (phones).
New: NightBlackout · Must
