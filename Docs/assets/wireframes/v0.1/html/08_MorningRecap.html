Flow, motion and edge cases
Flow
Night ends, then dawn transition (sky warms, lamp clicks on, puppets open eyes and stretch), then the recap note, then the Rondo (Day 6+), then the first Day turn.
Motion
Note slides up from the bottom and settles with a slight rotation; each line writes in one after another (0.15 s apart) with a typewriter sound.
Empty nights
If nothing happened to you: a short line, 'Quiet night. Nothing missing.', shown for 2 s only.
Phone vs PC
Same. Note width 80% of the screen on phones.
