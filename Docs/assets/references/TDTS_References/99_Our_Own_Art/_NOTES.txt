WHAT GOES HERE
OUR team's own drawings only (Barros, Villa, others). These are the ONLY files
Claude may trace, vectorize or turn into game art.

  cards_fronts/       card illustrations: 4 Supply kinds, 5 Morning cards
  cards_backs/        Supply, Trash, Flashlight, Night Skip, Day Skip, Bunker Key back
  characters_parts/   puppet parts per the C15 spec (same canvas, line and color separate)
  environments/       bunker room, street, lobby, night sketches
  brand/              Silong Shelters sun mascot and poster sketches

Best quality for tracing: the largest size you have (at least 1500 px tall),
PNG, plain or transparent background, line art and color as separate files
if you have them. Phone photos of paper work too; shoot flat, in daylight.

ONE LINE PER FILE:
filename.png - artist - what it is - anything to keep exactly (e.g. "keep the wobbly lines")
