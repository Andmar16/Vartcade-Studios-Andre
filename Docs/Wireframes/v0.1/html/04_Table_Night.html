Night flow, motion and privacy
Night turn flow
Night banner, Peek (required), then a second action or Peek again; both pips used, 'Lights out' toast, next player. While others act, you see the blackout screen (board 05).
Motion
Peek: the target card tilts up toward the camera and flips (0.4 s). Steal: card slides across the table into your fan, low and quiet. Swap: two cards cross paths mid-table.
Phone vs PC
Same as Day. The action column has 4 buttons, so on short phones it becomes 2 x 2.
Privacy
Nothing about another player's Night action is shown live to anyone. Results only appear in the morning recap (board 08).
Notes for developers
Game table: Night phase, your turn
1
Night environment
Lamp switches off with a click; moonlight through the window; the whole scene gets a teal tint and a dark vignette. Shadows of the lamp chain sway on the wall. Ambient: crickets, distant wind, a creak now and then.
TableBackdrop · Should (A10) · Transition 1.2 s
2
Everyone's eyes are closed
All puppets show closed eyes (face part swap) and slump slightly. This sells the 'eyes closed' rule from the tabletop game.
PlayerZoneController + puppet face · Must (C03)
3
Peek result panel
Peek is its own button and must be used once. Tap Peek, then tap any card in another hand: its back flips toward you only. Panel shows front and back, then the card keeps an eye marker for you for the rest of the game.
CardPicker + new PeekPanel · Must
4
Peeked marker
Small teal eye badge on cards you have peeked. Only you see it.
CardView · Must
5
Night action bar
2 action pips. Before the first Peek, Steal, Swap and Flashlight are disabled with the reason 'Peek first'. After a Steal or Swap, the other one locks ('One per Night'). Flashlight appears only if you hold one on a back.
ActionBarController · Must (L05)
6
Steal target highlight
Steal picks a specific card by its front (gold glow). Swap takes a random card, so it highlights the whole hand instead of one card.
CardPicker · Must
7
Your hand at Night
Same fan. The played Morning card shows USED (greyed, red stamp) but still counts at Sundown by its back.
HandController · Must
8
Portrait at Night
Your portrait also shows closed eyes while you are not acting; it opens its eyes only during your Night turn, as a reminder that you are 'awake'.
PortraitView · Should
9
Phase tag
Teal NIGHT tag. The turn order is the same as the Day.
HUDController · Must
10
Flashlight reveal
Flashlight (one use) shows every back in the chosen hand, including your own. Opens a wide panel with all backs in a row for 4 s, then closes.
New: FlashlightPanel · Must
